Channel routing

ES-9 default channel numbering: 1/2 main balanced outs (not DC-coupled), 3/4 headphone mix, 5/6 S/PDIF, 7/8 ES-5, 9–16 the DC-coupled 3.5mm Eurorack jacks — jack "Out 1" is channel 9, "Out 2" is channel 10, etc. Change these numbers here if you've remapped channels in the ES-9 config tool.

Calibration

Patch the CV jack into a scope or a known 1V/oct source and measure. The 3.5mm outs are rated ~±10V max; adjust until octaves land correctly on the Tonestar.
